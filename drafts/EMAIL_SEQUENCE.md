## Email 4 — The how-it-works breakdown · "What actually happens"
**Send:** Day 7
**Subject:** What Gradia actually does (plain English)

---

Hey [first name],

A few people have replied asking how this actually works — setup, what it does, what you have to do. Here's the short version.

**Setup (30–45 minutes, one time):**

You tell Gradia your shop name, your services and prices, and a couple sentences about how you work. That's it. No code, no integrations to configure, no account to connect to a phone system — it uses a business number we provision for you.

**What it does from there:**

The voice agent answers every inbound call to that number, 24/7. It introduces itself as part of your shop ("Thanks for calling [Shop Name]..."), listens to what the caller needs, quotes the job from your price list, and books the appointment on your calendar if the caller says yes.

The chat agent watches your CRM. When a new lead comes in, it drafts a follow-up text and email. When an old lead has gone quiet, it flags them and drafts a revival message. When you have an appointment in two days, it drafts a reminder.

All of that goes into an approvals queue. You see every message before it sends. You approve what you want. Nothing goes out without you.

**What you do:**

Check your approvals once a day. Tap approve on what looks good. Edit anything you want to change. That's your daily Gradia workflow.

**What it costs:**

$20/month — Core plan. Includes the CRM, calendar, chat agent, Whisper, and Agentic mode. Voice Receptionist (your business phone number + the answering agent) is a +$29/month add-on. Full stack: $49/month.

Founding 100: $10/month for life (Core only, voice add-on pricing TBD at launch).

**What it doesn't do:**

It doesn't post to Instagram or Facebook. It doesn't handle invoices or payments. It doesn't send anything without your approval. There is no "set it and forget it" — you stay in control.

See you on the 10th,
Harry

**From line:** "Harry at Gradia" — not "The Gradia Team." This is a founder-led launch.

**Pricing note:** Email 4 includes "Voice Receptionist +$29/mo" — this should only be sent once the acceptance run passes and the voice add-on is officially claimable. If the run hasn't passed by send time, replace with "Voice Receptionist pricing announced at launch."
