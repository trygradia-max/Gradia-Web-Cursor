## The moment: "Hear it answer a real call"

This script is built around the highest-proof demo moment Gradia has: a real phone call coming in after hours, being answered in seconds, quoted on the call, and booked straight to a calendar. That's the hook. Everything else in 60 seconds is just payoff.

## Director's notes

**Voice for the voiceover:** Not a polished announcer. Record in a real shop or garage if possible. Ideally the founder's voice, or a detailer's. Rough is better than slick — the audience trusts it more.

**Gradia's voice agent (Aria):** Should sound professional but natural, not robotic. The call audio should be a real demo recording, not synthesized for the video.

**Pacing:** The call sequence (0:05–0:22) is the heart of this video. Do NOT rush it. Viewers need to hear the actual conversation to believe it. The rest can be quick cuts.

**Music:** Subtle ambient. Not a hype track. Nothing that competes with the call audio.

**On-screen text:** Minimal. The UI does the work. Only caption essential moments (the booking, the approval button).

**Mobile-first framing:** If this posts to Instagram Reels or TikTok, put the most important visual (the phone call pickup) in the first 3 seconds. Can recut as a 15-second vertical with just the call + booking for paid ads.
